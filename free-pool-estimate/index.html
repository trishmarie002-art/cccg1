Common questions

What homeowners want to know.

How much does a custom swimming pool cost?

Cost depends on pool dimensions, design, site conditions, access, equipment, decking, and finishes. Request an estimate so the team can discuss your specific project.

Do you build pools in Clearwater and Tampa Bay?

CCCG serves Clearwater and the surrounding Tampa Bay area. Enter your project ZIP code so the team can confirm whether your property is within its service area.

Can you help with the pool design and permit process?

CCCG discusses design, site requirements, engineering, and required permits as part of pool construction planning. The exact scope is established for each project.

Is the estimate request free?

Yes. The initial estimate request is free and carries no obligation to hire CCCG.
