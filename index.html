BEFORE WE BUILD

Frequently Asked Questions

A few answers to help you plan your next project.

What types of construction projects do you handle?+

We handle residential and commercial construction, home remodeling, kitchens, bathrooms, roofing, additions, decks, excavation, new construction, and custom pool construction.

Are you licensed and insured? What are your license numbers?+

Yes, Coast to Coast Construction Group is fully licensed and insured across Florida. We hold licenses CGC1518966 (General), CMC057196 (Mechanical), CCC1330430 (Roofing), CUC12255160 (Utility/Excavation), and NASCLA commercial accreditation.

Where is your office located and what are your hours?+

Our office is located at 140 Island Way office #284, Clearwater, FL 33767. We are open Monday – Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Closed Saturday & Sunday). Reach us at (727) 922-7630 or Office@cccg-us.com.

How do I request an estimate?+

Call (727) 922-7630 or visit our Free Estimate page to tell us about your project. Estimates are personalized and free, with no obligations.

How long does construction take?+

Timing depends on the project’s size, complexity, permitting, and material availability. We discuss an estimated timeline during consultation.
