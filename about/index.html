Three Generations. One Family. Built to Last.

Coast to Coast Construction Group is more than a construction company - it's the result of three generations of hard work, family, and a commitment to doing things the right way.

Our story began with Herb Sr., who established an air conditioning installation and service company alongside his son, Herb Jr., laying the foundation for what would become a family tradition in the trades.

Herb Jr. soon earned his own air conditioning license and took over the family business. Over the years, he continued to expand his experience and capabilities, eventually earning his General Contractor license and opening the door to a much broader range of construction work.

That same passion for building was passed down to Herb's son, Andrew. From a young age, Andrew took a genuine interest in construction, growing up around the business and learning firsthand from his family. That passion eventually led to Coast to Coast Construction Group.

Today, Herb and Andrew run Coast to Coast side by side, carrying forward the foundation that began three generations ago while continuing to grow what the family can offer. With licenses spanning general contracting, air conditioning, roofing, pools and underground utilities, the company has grown considerably - but the values behind it haven't changed.

At the end of the day, we're still a family business. We believe in taking care of our customers, standing behind our work, and building something our family - and yours - can be proud of.
