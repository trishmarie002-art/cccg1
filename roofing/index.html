PLAN WITH CONFIDENCE

Your questions, answered.

Should I repair or replace my roof?+

That depends on its condition, age, the extent of damage, and the existing roof system. A project review helps determine the appropriate scope.

What should I do if I notice a leak?+

Call to arrange a review and share where you see water entering. Avoid climbing onto the roof yourself.

How long will roofing work take?+

Timing depends on roof size, the agreed scope, material availability, weather, and any permitting requirements. Discuss a project-specific schedule at your estimate.

Do you offer warranties?+

Ask about applicable workmanship and manufacturer warranty terms for your specific materials and scope before work begins.
